Turn it on in System Settings

    macOS says the screensaver “can’t be opened”?

    That’s Apple’s check for apps outside the App Store, and nothing is wrong. Click Done, open Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Or paste this in Terminal and press Return:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Screen\ Savers/Mantel.saver
