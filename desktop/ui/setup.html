Set up your screensaver

Four quick steps. We’ll tell you exactly what to click.
1

Sign in to Mantel

Checking…

2

Install the screensaver

Adds Mantel to your Mac’s list of screensavers.

3

Connect it to your account

Points the screensaver at your photos and looks.

If macOS asks “Mantel would like to access data from other apps”, click Allow. That’s how Mantel saves your screensaver’s settings. It can’t read anything else.
4

Choose Mantel in System Settings

Click below, scroll to Other, and pick Mantel. To set how soon it starts, go to Lock Screen → “Start Screen Saver when inactive”.

If macOS says the screensaver “can’t be opened” or is from an unidentified developer: that’s Apple’s check for apps outside the App Store, and nothing is wrong. Click Done, then open Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Or paste this in Terminal and press Return:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Screen\ Savers/Mantel.saver
